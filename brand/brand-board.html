Brand board · v1
The vibe

Warm, clean,
honest, chill.

Builder, student, training most mornings. Warm-clean posts for words, moody-warm photos for life, and one Imigongo motif from Rwanda to make it mine. No hustle talk, no guru posts, no quote cards.

01 · Logo

The Amatana diamond

Two nested bands around a caramel core, taken straight from Imigongo's Amatana motif, next to SHAY in calm tall caps. Use the diamond alone anywhere it's small: profile picture, favicon, watermark.

02 · Colour

Chocolate, caramel, olive, oat

Oat and chocolate do most of the work. Olive is the second surface. Caramel is the small thing: the diamond core, the border, one number. Night is only for moody photo frames and end cards.

05 · Photos

Warm, natural, a little grain

Natural light, golden hour, gym lights, real places. Grade everything with the Shay LUTs: Warm for daytime and clean posts, Low Light for night, gym and moody reels. In CapCut, start at 70–80% intensity.

Left to right: original, Shay Warm, Shay Low Light. Shown on a reference feed you shared, for comparison only; rebuild with your own photos to replace it.

Photo first, words second

The photo fills the frame; the caption sits on a solid night or oat band below it, never on top of busy parts.

Frame, don't filter

Oat margins around a photo turn it into a page. Use it for carousel covers and text-heavy posts.

Keep the safe zone

Reels and stories: keep words in the middle 4:5 so the grid crop and the UI never cut them.
